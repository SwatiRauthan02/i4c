These trailing stops, combined with minimum rank entry rules lead to the imposition of 'go-to-cash' and 'stay-in-cash' rules if there is a broad market bearish turn. Open Position Trailing stop levels are quoted with a percentage that indicates the offset from the most recent close price. Green indicates the trailing stop is "above water".

  • Exit Price reflects a price that an active and experienced trader would achieve when following stops that are updated prior to every session.
  • Max Move is defined as the highest high achieved during the holding period.
  • Annualized returns reflect compounded daily excess returns above risk-free rate, assuming 240 trading days per year.
